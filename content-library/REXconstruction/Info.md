## Services and service area

The official Services page describes roofing and exterior construction, including roof replacements, repairs, and inspections; gutters; siding; windows; and interior painting through subcontractor coordination. The company describes a three-stage process:

1. **Pre-construction:** paperwork, photo documentation, scheduling, weather monitoring, material delivery coordination, and homeowner updates.
2. **Construction:** coordinating and holding roofing, gutter, window, and interior-painting subcontractors accountable; managing sequence, production, and quality control.
3. **Post-construction:** site cleanup, final inspection, finishing work, and a final walkthrough.

- **Primary area named on the Services page:** Raleigh, Wake Forest, Clayton, Cary, and Garner, North Carolina.
- **Other markets mentioned on the About page:** Wilmington and Minnesota. Confirm active coverage before describing these as current service areas.
- **Insurance restoration:** The site says the company specializes in the insurance restoration process and helps customers understand carrier requirements for storm-related claims.
- **Inspection offer:** The Contact page invites visitors to request a free roof inspection for storm damage or repair needs.

- The site emphasizes clear communication, budgeting, staffing, on-site organization, quality workmanship, and thorough cleanup.
- The company is named after Rex, a Plott hound found as a puppy in Wilmington after Hurricane Florence. The site connects cleanup practices to keeping yards safer for children and animals.
