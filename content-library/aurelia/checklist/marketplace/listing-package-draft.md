## Long description

AURELIA is built for intimate restaurants, chef-led concepts, and supper clubs. It pairs an editorial visual system with a story-led browsing experience: introduce the atmosphere, reveal the menu, share the people behind the kitchen, and guide guests toward a reservation request.

The template includes Home, Menu, About, Chefs, Reserve, and a custom 404 page. It uses shared styles, reusable native Framer components, responsive Desktop/Tablet/Phone layouts, expandable dish and chef stories, and an editable reservation-request form.

The project ships with fictional demo content so you can replace the restaurant name, copy, imagery, menu details, and booking destination without inheriting real operational claims.

## Included

## Editing and feature notes

- The seven-dish systems are deliberately fixed-item components, not CMS collections. They are best when the menu needs a curated, stable sequence.
- Replace demo imagery, copy, contact details, booking destination, favicon, social image, and all fictional operational facts before publishing a real restaurant site.
- The reservation form requires your own submission destination, privacy language, and success/error policy before production use.
- Custom image-bearing components and the Home Hero now have explicit Alt handling. Do not claim full accessibility readiness until the cursor-preview Alt decision, keyboard/Focus, Reduced Motion, contrast, and final regression tests are verified.

## Support statement

Support covers template setup, normal Framer editing, and reproducible template issues. It does not include custom copywriting, third-party booking-service configuration, legal/privacy advice, bespoke development, or licensing assets supplied by a buyer.
