# Golden Moment

## Title

Golden Moment

---

## Subtitle

Two small frames of yellow, seen from beneath the branches and then through the light above.

---

## Sections

### Under the Tree

I stood under the tree and looked up.  
The leaves held the afternoon in small yellow pieces,  
with enough sky between them for the moment to keep breathing.

### Above the Tree

Above, the same yellow becomes almost too bright for the film.  
The details thin out,  
and what remains is closer to a feeling than a record.
