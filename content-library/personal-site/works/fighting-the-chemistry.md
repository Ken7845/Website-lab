# Fighting the Chemistry

## Title

Fighting the Chemistry

---

## Subtitle

A story of my battle against the volatile chemistry of instant film in midsummer.

---

## Body

The first frame. I'm after a specific green -- the deep, saturated green of this tree against a summer sky. The instant film decides otherwise. Summer light is indiscriminate; it floods everything. The image bleaches out before I can stop it.
