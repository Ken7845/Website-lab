# Payment SOP

## Current Payment Options

1. PayPal Invoice
2. Wise / Bank Transfer
3. Stripe or Airwallex Payment Link, later stage

## Pricing

Starter Demo Site: $300–500
Standard Site: $700–1200
Premium Site: $1500+

## Payment Terms

- 50% deposit before production
- 50% final payment before publishing / handover
- Includes 2 revision rounds
- Extra changes billed separately

## Client Records

For each client, keep:

- Client name
- Business name
- Email
- Invoice
- Payment screenshot / receipt
- Delivery link
- Revision notes
